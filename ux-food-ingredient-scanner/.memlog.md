# Memory Log - Food Ingredient Scanner UX

## Decisions

### User & Stakes
- **Primary users**: Everyone seeking healthier eating - individuals, parents, people with medical conditions (allergies, diabetes)
- **Brand direction**: Fresh & Modern, welcoming and attractive
- **Platform**: iOS first
- **Stakes**: Medical sensitivity - users with conditions that REQUIRE dietary changes

### Core Experience
- **Day 1 flow**: Quick Product Scan - scan and get instant safety feedback
- **Onboarding**: Personalized Setup before first scan
- **Profiles**: Family Overview with separate profiles for family members
- **Usage context**: Dual Mode (in-store + at-home research)
- **Result depth**: Educational Focus with deep dives, alternatives, health context

### Safety & Tone
- **Approach**: Gentle Guidance - supportive language, educational focus, less alarming visuals
- **Medical handling**: Respectful for serious conditions without being fear-based

### Premium Features
- Family profiles with different dietary needs
- Smart recommendations and healthier alternatives
- Advanced analytics and personalized tips
- Unlimited avoid list items

### Visual Direction
- **Primary color**: Fresh Green (#10B981) - health, natural, organic feel
- **Safety colors**: Gentle approach - light backgrounds with dark text
- **Typography**: SF Pro Display (iOS native)
- **Design system**: Material Design 3 adapted for iOS

### Key Flows
1. Quick scan in grocery aisle (< 3 seconds to result)
2. First-time setup with health profile (< 2 minutes)
3. Family profile management (switch in < 3 seconds)
4. Educational deep dives for ingredients
